📋 UML Worksheet — Aling Nena Adds Deliveries

OOP II · Section 8 Try It. A mutated domain: complete the UML, defend every edge, then reason about a change by tracing arrows.

The New Feature (the brief)

Customers are busy, so Aling Nena starts deliveries: a driver (name, motorcycle plate) picks up a completed sale and bikes it home for a fee. Each delivery is for exactly one sale; a sale gets delivered at most once. A driver can carry many deliveries over the week, and deliveries must be tracked as pending or delivered — and only a pending delivery can be marked delivered.

📌 Existing classes stay: Store, Product, PerishableProduct, Sale. Your job is to add the new pieces and redraw the whole picture.

Legend — the notation toolbox
